# Phase 01: Experience Storyboard

## Core Concept
**PRECISION IN MOTION**
A premium industrial technology experience. It focuses on the raw industrial utility combined with high-end digital precision.

---

## 01: DISCOVER
**Grade X is different.**

* **Purpose:** Establish immediate authority, high-tech positioning, and differentiate Grade X from generic cleaning companies.
* **Message:** "Precision Industrial Hygiene. Engineered for Commercial Kitchens."
* **Visual:** A striking, dark, high-contrast hero environment. A subtle, slow-moving 3D focal element (e.g., a stylized metallic turbine or the robotic chassis) resting in shadows, illuminated by sharp cyan/blue laser-like scanning lights.
* **Interaction:** Mouse movement slightly shifts the lighting angle on the 3D element (parallax/lighting shift).
* **Transition:** Scroll triggers a dramatic zoom *into* the 3D element, transitioning from the pristine exterior into a dark, confined space.
* **CTA:** Primary: "Discover Our Process" (anchor link down).
* **Mobile Behavior:** Device gyroscope controls the subtle lighting shift instead of mouse movement. The zoom-in transition on scroll remains smooth but optimized.

---

## 02: SEE
**The problem exists inside the exhaust system.**

* **Purpose:** Expose the hidden danger of grease buildup in inaccessible commercial kitchen exhausts.
* **Message:** "What you can't see is your biggest risk. Commercial exhausts hide dangerous buildup."
* **Visual:** The environment becomes dark, textured, and claustrophobic. Representing the inside of an exhaust duct. Volumetric lighting and particle dust. A grimy, heavily textured surface comes into focus.
* **Interaction:** As the user scrolls down, the viewport pans along the "duct," revealing the extent of the invisible problem.
* **Transition:** A sharp, horizontal scan-line (laser effect) sweeps down the screen, wiping away the darkness and transitioning to the solution.
* **CTA:** None (narrative progression).
* **Mobile Behavior:** Vertical pan on scroll. The visual is constrained to a tighter frame to maintain the claustrophobic feel.

---

## 03: UNDERSTAND
**Robotic technology provides access and visibility.**

* **Purpose:** Introduce the robotic technology as the unique differentiator that solves the access problem.
* **Message:** "Unrestricted Access. Unprecedented Visibility. Driven by Robotics."
* **Visual:** The Grade X robot comes into view within the duct. A sleek, rugged machine with active camera lights piercing the darkness. 
* **Interaction:** Scroll progress scrubs through a 3D sequence of the robot extending its arms or turning its camera head toward the user.
* **Transition:** The robot's camera flashes or beams a bright light, blowing out the screen to white/cyan for a brief moment.
* **CTA:** None (narrative progression).
* **Mobile Behavior:** Simplified 3D sequence or a high-quality pre-rendered GSAP scrubbed video to ensure performance, keeping the focus on the robot's capabilities.

---

## 04: MEASURE
**Grade X documents grease thickness.**

* **Purpose:** Highlight the data-driven, precise nature of the Grade X process before cleaning begins.
* **Message:** "Precision Measurement. We map the exact thickness of grease deposits."
* **Visual:** A digital overlay/HUD appears over the duct environment. Wireframes, topographic mappings of the grease, and numerical data points (e.g., "Grease Depth: 4.2mm", "Combustibility: High") appear dynamically.
* **Interaction:** Hovering over specific data points expands them into detailed tooltips with technical readouts.
* **Transition:** The digital overlay collapses into a targeted focal point where the cleaning process begins.
* **CTA:** None.
* **Mobile Behavior:** Data points animate in sequentially on scroll rather than relying on hover interactions.

---

## 05: CLEAN
**The system is professionally cleaned.**

* **Purpose:** Demonstrate the effectiveness and thoroughness of the cleaning process.
* **Message:** "Industrial-Grade Eradication. Restoring systems to bare metal."
* **Visual:** A dynamic visual of the cleaning in action. High-pressure steam or robotic brush action clearing the previously mapped grease. A stark contrast between the grimy "before" and the polished "after."
* **Interaction:** A custom slider or a scroll-bound scrub that allows the user to manually control the "wipe" between the dirty and clean states of the metal surface.
* **Transition:** The camera pulls back out of the duct, returning to a wider, cleaner, and brighter industrial space.
* **CTA:** None.
* **Mobile Behavior:** Touch-drag interaction for the before/after slider.

---

## 06: VERIFY
**Results are inspected and documented.**

* **Purpose:** Reinforce accountability, compliance, and the guarantee of a job done perfectly.
* **Message:** "Verified to ISO Standards. No shortcuts, just bare metal."
* **Visual:** Clean, white/cyan aesthetic. A glowing "verified" badge or stamp of compliance. Data visualizations showing 0.0mm remaining buildup.
* **Interaction:** Scrolling cascades a series of digital checkmarks and ISO/compliance icons.
* **Transition:** Smooth fade and slide up into the final reporting dashboard preview.
* **CTA:** "View Compliance Standards" (secondary button).
* **Mobile Behavior:** Cards cascade from the bottom on scroll, stacking neatly.

---

## 07: PROVE
**The client receives evidence and reporting.**

* **Purpose:** Show the tangible deliverable the client receives, ensuring peace of mind.
* **Message:** "Total Transparency. Comprehensive digital reporting delivered instantly."
* **Visual:** A sleek mockup of the Grade X digital reporting dashboard (tablet/mobile frames) floating in 3D space, displaying high-res before/after photos and certification documents.
* **Interaction:** Hovering over the dashboard tilts it in 3D space and highlights different sections of the report (e.g., Photos, Certificate, Recommendations).
* **Transition:** The dashboard dissolves as the final CTA block expands to fill the screen.
* **CTA:** "Download Sample Report" (ghost/outline button).
* **Mobile Behavior:** A horizontal scroll/swipe carousel of the report features instead of 3D hover tilts.

---

## 08: ACT
**Request a quote.**

* **Purpose:** Convert the convinced user into a lead.
* **Message:** "Secure Your Infrastructure. Get a precise quote today."
* **Visual:** A minimalist, premium footer area. High contrast (dark background with glowing brand accents). Clean typography and a streamlined contact/quote form.
* **Interaction:** Input fields have sharp, micro-interactions on focus (glowing borders, crisp label animations). 
* **Transition:** End of page.
* **CTA:** "Request a Quote" (Solid, highly visible primary button).
* **Mobile Behavior:** Form is stacked vertically, inputs optimized for mobile keyboards. Standard footer behavior.
