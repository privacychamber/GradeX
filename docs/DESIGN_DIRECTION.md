# Phase 00: Design Direction Document

## Theme: Precision in Motion
The website should feel like a premium industrial technology experience. It must evoke trust, transparency, and high-tech verification.

### Core Narrative
"You cannot easily see what is happening inside a commercial kitchen exhaust system."
Grade X can:
* **INSPECT**
* **MEASURE**
* **CLEAN**
* **VERIFY**
* **REPORT**

### Anti-Goals (What to avoid)
* Cleaning company templates.
* Generic SaaS aesthetic.
* Overly cyberpunk or "AI-generated" futuristic looks.
* WebGL demos with no purpose.
* Generic corporate websites.

### Guidelines
* Focus on raw industrial utility combined with high-end digital precision.
* The design should facilitate the 5-step core narrative clearly.
* Typography and layout must be extremely deliberate, resembling a high-grade technical instrument panel or a precision engineering brochure.
